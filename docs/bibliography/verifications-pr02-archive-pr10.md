**Correction of an error of the card `BLOQ-12`** (6 October): the card wrote « en roues `x/0 = ⊥` donc `1/0 = ⊥` » ("in wheels `x/0 = ⊥` hence `1/0 = ⊥`"). This is false: in a wheel, `0/0 = ⊥` (nullity, absorbing for addition) and `1/0 = ∞` (unsigned infinity). The valid counterexample is that IEEE 754 has **two** signed infinities. The text of the manuscript (`thm:homomorphisme_roues`) has been corrected accordingly. [Wheel theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheel_theory).
