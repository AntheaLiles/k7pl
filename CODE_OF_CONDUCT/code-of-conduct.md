# Code of Conduct template

Use this document when revising the adopted policy in [../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Do not maintain a second divergent public policy here.

**Document version:** 1.0.0  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-06  
**Audience:** maintainers preparing a revision.

## Purpose

State community standards, scope, reporting route, enforcement responsibilities, and attribution.

## Community commitment

Describe expected respectful, inclusive, and professional behaviour.

## Expected behaviour

List concrete behaviours that contribute to a healthy community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

List concrete behaviours that are not acceptable, including harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disclosure of private information without permission.

## Scope

Define project spaces and circumstances in which the policy applies.

## Reporting

Provide the current private reporting route and confidentiality expectations.

## Enforcement

Define responsible roles, fair and proportionate response principles, and possible consequences.

## Attribution

Identify the upstream code-of-conduct source and version and preserve required attribution and licensing information.

## Review

Record the next review date and reason. Keep the adopted policy itself in the root CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md.
